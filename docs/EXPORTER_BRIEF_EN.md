# A chamber-convened confidential emissions round

**Prospective briefing - no chamber, regulator or association has joined this
prototype. All current reports, actors and sector totals are SIMULATED.**

An exporter group could jointly produce an independently checkable weighted
emissions figure without giving each competitor the others' complete reports.
Each installation would retain its report locally, reconcile the applicable
measurement boundary and activity unit, obtain proper verification, and seal its
reviewed inputs. Independent trustees would receive encrypted threshold shares.
An agreed public round would freeze the contributing set and check the aggregate
against those commitments before publishing it.

The chamber's proposed role is convening and process administration: identify the
eligible population, obtain consent, resolve data definitions, appoint genuinely
independent operators and explain missing members. It should not silently choose
a favourable sample or treat its own production estimate as an audited national
denominator. Two colluding trustees can recover individual inputs; that residual
risk must be accepted explicitly and addressed through custody and governance.

The output would be a signed/provenance-linked evidence dossier and reproducible
arithmetic, not an accredited verification opinion or automatic regulatory
recognition. [CBAM Annex IV point 7](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/2083/oj/eng)
provides a possible regional-evidence route; the competent process still decides
reliability and acceptance. [CBAM calculation rules](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2025/2547/oj/eng)
and [TR-ETS pilot procedures](https://iklim.gov.tr/db/turkce/duyurular/files/Yay%C4%B1mlanan_%20TR%20ETS%20Usul%20ve%20Esaslar.pdf)
require programme-specific definitions that a generic average does not replace.

A first engagement should be a shadow exercise with no compliance or allocation
effect: establish a reference workflow, agree the full population and units,
measure review effort and errors, compare exact totals, assess privacy and publish
limitations. Funding, sites, accountable providers and a start date remain
unagreed. See [the pilot plan](PILOT_PLAN.md) and [limits](LIMITATIONS.md).
