## Boundary reconciliation before use

An official-use adapter must identify the installation/sub-installation and
eligible population, period, product category, functional unit, emissions scope,
activity attribution, precursor treatment, factor sources, assurance opinion and
denominator authority. Reviewers must prevent double counting and explain missing
members. The current generic country/product/year parameters do not implement all
of those sector-specific definitions; kg product is not every programme's unit.

The association's 0.88 declaration and the EU fallback are not asserted to share
identical accounting boundaries. The calculator explicitly leaves regional
mark-up treatment UNVERIFIED and shows both assumptions. It does not calculate
allocation adjustments, origin carbon-price deductions or payable liability.
Neither a lower assumed default nor a fee saving is a measured emissions reduction.

No official reporting account, registry API, TYS submission or accredited verifier
connection is integrated. The dossier is an engineering evidence attachment for
review, not an official submission or a standards-compliance certificate.
