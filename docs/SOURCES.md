# Sources and claim register

Access date for every retrieved source: **2026-10-21 (Asia/Kolkata)**. Retrieval used Firecrawl with `maxAge: 0`. This records an access attempt and returned content, not a guarantee that the underlying publisher has no later amendments. Status describes evidence quality, not legal advice or acceptance.

`data/real/facts.json` stores machine-readable facts and source IDs. `data/real/evidence/` contains selected source passages or bounded research notes with URLs and access dates. `SHA256SUMS` records hashes of these saved evidence files, not of the original web pages or full retrieved responses. Official law excerpts retain original decimal commas and table headers. These are selected access records, not an archive of complete source documents. Calculations and engineering parameters are explicitly separate from external facts. Search snippets alone do not establish a rule.

## Validation and local model source preparation (accessed 2026-10-22)

- **S23** [IPCC 2006 Vol 2 Ch 2 stationary combustion](https://www.ipcc-nggip.iges.or.jp/public/2006gl/pdf/2_Volume2/V2_2_Ch2_Stationary_Combustion.pdf) - Table 2.3 manufacturing and construction: kg CO2/TJ on a net calorific basis. Petroleum coke 97500 (82900-115000); other bituminous coal 94600 (89500-99700); natural gas 56100 (54300-58300); lignite 101000 (90900-115000). These are defaults, not plant-specific measured factors.
- **S24** [IPCC 2006 Vol 3 Ch 2 mineral industry](https://www.ipcc-nggip.iges.or.jp/public/2006gl/pdf/3_Volume3/V3_2_Ch2_Mineral_Industry.pdf) - Clinker rather than cement production is needed for clinker process CO2. CaO origin, non-carbonate inputs, CKD, MgO, and carbon capture affect methodology. Tier 2 and 3 account for CKD. A simple carbonate stoichiometric plausibility check does not constitute a complete monitoring methodology.
- **S25** [EU cement BAT conclusions, Decision 2013/163/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32013D0163) - Section 1.2.3 Table 1: 2900-3300 MJ/tonne clinker is a BAT-associated energy consumption range for new plants and major upgrades using dry-process multistage preheating and precalcination, subject to raw-material moisture and footnotes. It is not a universal physical feasibility band.
- **S26** [IPCC cement production background paper](https://www.ipcc-nggip.iges.or.jp/public/gp/bgp/3_1_Cement_Production.pdf) - Section 2.2.1 gives EF_clinker = fractionCaO * (44.01 g/mol CO2 / 56.08 g/mol CaO). It discusses non-carbonate CaO, lost CKD, clinker imports/exports, confidential information and external review. Do not use its historical activity data as a current fact.

- **S29** [NIST calcium oxide](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C1305788&Units=SI) - NIST Chemistry WebBook species calcium oxide, CaO, CAS 1305-78-8, molecular weight 56.077. Rounded to two decimals: 56.08.

## Phase 7 primary scope review (accessed 2026-10-22)

- **S15** [CBAM calculation and attribution methods](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2025/2547/oj/eng) — IR 2025/2547, Articles 3–6: system boundaries, functional units, monitoring plan and attribution. Article 4(5) uses tonnes of clinker contained in specified cement goods. Direct-only simulated E is not a full CBAM embedded-emissions result.
- **S32** [ISO 14064-1:2018 public scope](https://www.iso.org/standard/66453.html) — Organisation-level GHG quantification, reporting and inventory management. Programme-specific requirements remain additional. Public scope only; no licensed clause text or conformance certification.
- **S33** [ISO 14064-3:2019 public scope](https://www.iso.org/standard/66455.html) — Principles and requirements for validation/verification of organisation, project and product GHG statements. Programme requirements remain additional; a cryptographic signature is not a verification engagement.
- **S34** [ISO 14065:2020 public scope](https://www.iso.org/standard/74257.html) — Requirements for bodies validating/verifying environmental information; a sector application of ISO/IEC 17029:2019. Correct designation is ISO 14065, not ISO/IEC 14065. An allowlisted public key establishes no accreditation.
- **S35** [Wüst and Gervais, Do you need a Blockchain?](https://eprint.iacr.org/2017/375.pdf) — Original decision framework, Section III and Figure 1: storage, multiple writers, acceptable online trusted party, writer trust/identity and public verifiability. Known mutually distrusting writers can fit a public permissioned design; public permissionless settlement is a separate project choice.
