# Prospective pilot plan

**Not launched. No partner, funding or site is committed.** This proposal follows
the eight launch-definition fields in Pillar 1 of the [COP31 AI-Enabled Cleantech
concept note](https://cop31.tr/assets/docs/action-agenda/cop31-ai-enabled-cleantech-concept-note.pdf)
(S11, read 2026-10-21/22). A prototype and a list of possible partners do not meet
that definition. All durations and targets below are proposed engineering choices.

## Challenge owner

To be agreed with a chamber/industry convenor and the relevant public authority.
The candidate challenge is producing reviewable weighted industrial-emissions
evidence while restricting disclosure of commercially sensitive plant records.
DoCC, TURKCIMENTO and a chamber are possible stakeholders, not participating
partners or operators of this demo.

## Technology provider

The Ortalama project would provide the protocol, local extraction/review tooling,
public verifier and export format. A contracted accountable provider, maintenance
responsibilities, independent security reviewer and standards/MRV specialist must
be selected before launch. No sponsor supplies a special SDK or certifies the build.

## Implementation site

Proposed: a consented, homogeneous clinker sub-installation cohort with a defined
system year and an independently reconciled eligible population. Sites and real
reports are not available. Begin with a sandbox; real-report work requires
separate institutional trustee custody and reviewed data-handling agreements.

## Agreed workplan

Proposed eight-week sequence: two weeks for legal scope, population/units,
baseline and consent; two for endpoint hardening, identity/key custody and audited
protocol/setup changes; two for shadow runs against a controlled reference
calculation; two for independent evaluation, privacy-release review and a joint
decision on further use. All parties must agree the plan, responsibilities and
stop conditions. Live allocation or compliance decisions are excluded from the
initial shadow exercise.

## Baseline

Before intervention, record the approved comparison workflow, analyst effort,
time to reconcile a cohort, errors/corrections, data disclosures and independently
calculated reference totals. Do not use a self-declared national average as ground
truth or attribute national production to the voluntary cohort. The existing
20-report synthetic set is a software evaluation, not this operational baseline.

## Impact measurement plan

Compare exact integer totals and rational intensity with the agreed reference;
require zero unexplained arithmetic differences and complete provenance for all
included inputs. Separately measure extraction failures, analyst correction time,
false blocks, review quality, trustee availability, privacy incidents and user
comprehension. Report cohort coverage against the agreed denominator, with an
account of excluded facilities. Independent reviewers must approve public claims.

Measure model/service power and elapsed work, an agreed location/time electricity
factor, relevant embodied assumptions and uncertainty. Tokens and execution time
alone are not CO2e. Any physical emissions reduction needs its own counterfactual,
measurement boundary and assurance; a different default or carbon fee is not such
a reduction. These are proposed measures, not achieved outcomes.

## Financing pathway

To be negotiated: a paid discovery/shadow-MRV engagement, followed only if justified
by a costed institutional pilot. No budget, grant, investor, purchase agreement or
disbursement exists. Track expressions of interest separately from commitments,
contracts and disbursements, as the concept note requires.

## Implementation start date

**Unscheduled.** Start only after all eight fields are agreed, accountable roles
and financing are documented, real-data permissions are in place and the security,
identity, custody and regulatory gaps are resolved. No COP31 date is invented as
a commitment or acceptance milestone.

## Pillar 3 safeguards

Maintain the [AI card](AI_CARD.md), [footprint record](FOOTPRINT.md),
[threat model](THREAT_MODEL.md) and [oracle boundary](ORACLE.md). Use local report
processing, least disclosure, independent trustee organisations, consented release
rules, retention/deletion schedules and incident response. Review repeated and
overlapping cohorts before publication. Provide human correction paths, accessible
interfaces and native Turkish review. Publish measured successes and failures
with uncertainty, not promotional estimates. This maps to the concept note's
responsible/sustainable AI, data governance, cybersecurity, confidentiality and
knowledge-exchange scope; it is not affiliation with the initiative.
